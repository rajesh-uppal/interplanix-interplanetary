# Lunar Exploration and Orbital Mapping

Lunar exploration is an important part of Interplanix's longer-term
Earth–Moon–Mars vision.

This research area will investigate the modelling and mapping of lunar
orbital environments and the trajectory intelligence required to support
spacecraft operating around the Moon.

## Initial Research Topics

- Lunar orbital environment modelling
- Lunar orbital mapping
- Spacecraft trajectory determination
- Lunar navigation and tracking
- Lunar orbital traffic awareness
- Simulation-based mission planning

## Development Status

This is an initial research topic carried forward from the earlier
Moon Colonization Roadmap project. It is not a claim of an operational
lunar mapping or mission-planning capability.

The technical scope and implementation approach will be developed through
research and engineering validation.
